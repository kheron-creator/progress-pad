MIND SWEEP · TICK IF DONE, OR DRAG ONTO A LINE

How to use the T Tap a dot to tick. Tap the words to edit. Double-tap a line to add a step there.
Drag a step up, down or onto another line. Swipe it left to remove.
Hold a step to select it (or Ctrl/⌘-click), then copy or move to the next line.
Drag a line's name sideways to reorder lines. Tap the name for more.
Hold a step and tap "★ Star" to make it glow. Tap the ★ to unstar.
Tap a line's name, then "Name a thread" to label the line and link its thread.
Finished sprints move to the Done line on the left. Tap one to see its steps or reopen it.
Tap the T to focus on next steps and starred steps only.
Zoom in and out with − and +, a pinch, or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll. "Fit" shows every line.
Tap a line's name, then "Solo" to see only that line, "Just these" to pick a few, or "Hide" to put one away. Hidden lines stay as small marks: tap one to bring it back.
Drag along the mini map under the T to travel.
Each line hangs from its day. Tap the day above a line (or the name, then "Change day") to move it. Drag a line's name next to another line to join that line's day. Missed days show as "Catch up".
Hold a step to give it a Date or Minutes, or make it a Trigger. Steps due today glow.
Tap a day, then "+ Marker" or "+ Note" to pin a flag or a sticky note on the T.
Keys: ← → move along the T, T today, F fit, S solo the last line you touched, Esc show all.
Lanes groups lines by colour. Replay shows what you ticked on a past day.
Hold a step and tap "Colour" to highlight it. Tap a line's name, then "Colour", to colour the whole line.
